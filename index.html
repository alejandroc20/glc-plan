What to say

“What's your best price on this car? How far under MSRP can you go?”
“Can you include the $7,500 Mercedes lease credit in the quote?”
“I'd like the full lease numbers in writing — $0 due at signing, 36 months, 12,000 miles a year.”
“I'm getting a quote from another dealer too, so I need your best offer in writing to compare.”

Don't do this

✕

✕
Don't fall for “this price is only good today.”A real deal is still there tomorrow.

Don't rush. You're comparing, not buying.

The one rule that matters most

Get it in writing

Before you leave, have them email you the full quote — or take a clear photo of it. That paper is what gives you power at the next dealer.
